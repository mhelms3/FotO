Backstory

Son of Victor Fidelus and Shalelu the Ranger, a half-elven farm boy who feels overlooked by his famous father and is quite skeptical of "heroes". He was trained in fighting, nature and religion by his mother, who encouraged him to leave home on an "elfspringer" to explore and find his place in the world after he spontaneously manifested the ability to shield himself, despite having no magical training.

He is currently on these travels of exploration, but dreams of returning home to the farm, to live a peaceful life. In the meantime, he will learn what he can and help out those around him.
