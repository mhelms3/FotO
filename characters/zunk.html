Backstory

Thrown overboard after being caught as a stowaway on a merchant ship, Zunk swam ashore near Osprey Cove about a year ago. They lived off their skills for a couple of weeks until Elder Ordwi took a chance (and pity) on them, and started giving Zunk odd jobs in exchange for occasional room and board at the pub, eventually leading to more gainful, honest employment. They worked the bar, and eventually became a reliable runner/messenger/forager around the area.

Used to being a mistrusted outsider, Zunk holds Ordwi dear as one of few people to ever show them kindness and trust, and quickly volunteered to help accompany and guide her on her journey.

[Through certainly no fault of their own, Zunk found themselves on the run from the local magistrate in Varisia (Magnlmar?), and hopped in a conveniently ajar crate by the docks. Next thing they knew, they were headed north on a ship.]
