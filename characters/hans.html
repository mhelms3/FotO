Backstory

Hans' father was a tinker and inventor, and among the many things he installed into their home was indoor plumbing. But alas, his civil engineering and architecture skills were lacking, and he did not see the problem with a centrally located bathroom on the second floor above the dining room. Amidst a 6yo Hans' efforts to empty his bowels, he began to see colorful spots in the air. He thought he was pushing too hard, but they coalesced and separated into a multicolored mesh of otherworldly characters. Hans reached out and traced them with his fingers. And as he did, sounds echoed in his mind that he then repeated aloud. His indulgence in the trance was short-lived, as he then farted with explosive force and shat a glob of acid into the shattered toilet. The acid ate through the floor and the whole pile of porcelain and refuse fell to the room below, taking a shocked and emotionally confused Hans along for the ride. Alas, his father was eating a warm bowl of porridge at the time, a mid-afternoon snack he would not soon forget. First, he was burned by the acid, then battered by the toilet, and finally insulted by its contents. Hans was mostly unharmed by the fall but thoroughly shaken. And when his father emerged screaming from beneath the debris, Hans lost control of his bladder… and peed in his father's face. On the bright side, the urine seemed to neutralize the acid and soothe some of the pain.

Hans spent the next three months believing he had holy piss and a second magical anus. The number of people he asked to stare at his asshole that summer was staggering. This was the origin of Hans fascination with the arcane and his passion for civic ordinance and architectural regulation.

Hans grew up in Sandpoint in Varisia. His natural talent in the arcane arts found him leaving at a young age by gnomish standards to study at the Academy of Applied Magic in Pezzack — a bastion of free will on the northern coast of Cheliax. His love of functional architecture, civic ordinance, and the political rumor mill inclined him towards the civic wizardry that Pezzack specializes in.

Newly graduated and a youthful 41 years of age, Hans decided to indulge one of his hobbies and go backpacking in the Iron Archipelago. Growing up in Sandpoint, the stories of the Old Light and other Thassilonian ruins were quick to the lips of bards and parents at their children’s bedside. Enamored with the ancient architectural marvels, Hans spent much of his free time studying up on the lore of old Thassilon, and the mysterious ruins of Old Xin-Edasseril piqued his interest, in particular. Besides, an island vacation sounded exotic. Maybe he’d even find a new lady-friend… the last one ghosted him (that’s what they call it at the academy when your date flees through the ethereal plane). Admittedly, Hans may have spent a bit too much time talking about the finer points of magical applications to municipal sewage treatment systems. But she wasn’t really his type, anyway.

Hans hasn’t kept up much with Sandpoint locals of late — his tinker father passed away in an explosion of the man’s own sparking not long before Hans left for Pezzack, and his mother left them both shortly after his birth — she needed more excitement to keep the bleach at bay. Apparently, an orderly family life was too boring… gnomes will be gnomes, as they say. But what could have possibly changed in that stagnant backwater?
