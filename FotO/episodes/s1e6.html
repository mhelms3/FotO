He regards me gratefully and we send him limping slowly on his way. He will use some, or maybe most on drink, I know, but he has had a bad day. Now for Ordwii and whatever else awaits. We head through the north door.

Sure enough, a bunk room as told, with little in it otherwise. And the far northern door did seem to be blocked from the other side and well closed. Holdouts perhaps, or even some of our friends? Tom sizes up the door, like he is expecting it to do something, shrugs, and mutters, “well, if that’s the way you want it”, and gives it a tremendous kick. The door splinters, crashing inwards and a stone bench behind it barricade flies across the room to smash into a stone table. The room has a lowish ceiling, hung candelabras sporting glowing mushrooms that give off a soft bluish glow revealing two man-froggy things inside. They stand like men, but their heads are big-eyed and froggy, and there is webbing between the clawed fingers and toes. We head down the hallway Queen Bogard indicated, passing a door we were told to avoid, and beheld a strange domed room lit by torches. A pair of massive columns of rusted metal rise in large alcoves to the east and south, each sporting a jumble of hinged armatures and drooping lengths of wire that connect them to one of two large metallic worktables. Gears grind slowly and glowing pulsing crystals pulse from these worktables. A large stone well stands in the center of the room between the two strange worktables, capped with a stone slab engraved with spikey horseshoe shaped rune we have seen on the doors and the acolyte’s robes. Three acolytes are busy at the tables, turning switches and knobs, but they are so engaged they do not notice Zunk and Hans creeping forwards around the strange pillars.

Zunk streaks out from behind the column, and by the time the acolyte notices the goblin it is too late. Zunk drops to the floor and slides past the surprised man, knives flashing, and gashes the acolyte, spilling guts and blood everywhere, except miraculously, on Zunk themself. Ughh, why is this always so disgusting? Tom takes advantage of the opening, swinging over the crouching Zunk and separates the acolyte’s head from the rest of him. More mess, and a single bright red drop splatters onto Zunk’s cheek.

The last acolyte looks up in alarm, and she turns dials furiously as Hans extends his arms and lets loose a blast of magical force that pushes her away, but not before she hits one last switch. There is a fizzing cracking sound as the well cap fissures, and a giant grub like thing now appears on the runed stone. It shines wetly, flashing rainbow colors like a fish glinting in the sun.

Ragnulf roars “Die you rusting bastards” and makes towards the last acolyte to take his revenge, hammer swinging. Ragnulf needs some aid, since he is not fully healed from his previous wounds, and this seems a good time to grant him such. Ragnulf exacts his revenge on the acolyte, aided by Zunk, and they turn their attention to the grub along with Tom.

We have it surrounded now, but the squishy thing is not much affected by the sharp blades, flesh oozing around to seal the cuts, so it goes after me. Blows land on my armor but do me no harm, while Hans tries a spell that the thing absorbs, healing it a little. A hammer from Ragnulf has some effect, and I beat down on the horrid thing again, splattering goop everywhere.
