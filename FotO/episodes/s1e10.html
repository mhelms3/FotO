Season 1, Episode 10: Finale Part 2: Pride Before the Fall.

Just then we were interrupted as Ordwii, Ragnulf and 3 others rushed into the chamber. I wasn’t certain why she had rethought her decision to help the town prepare, but I guess she must have been concerned for us. It would be just like her to rush to our aid. But then, what was Ragnulf doing here? He was iron-bound the last time I saw him, and set for trial.

“Oh my friends. I knew I was not wrong. Look at what you have wrought-so glorious!” Wait-what? This did not sound like something Ordwii would say-she was most distressed by violence. What is going on?

“Good to see you Ordwii” says Zunk, but I could sense something strange in his voice. “This happy greeting has reminded me of when we first met, and our first drink together and our toast over a mug of Barnell’s brown. Good times, yes?”

“uh, why yes of course” she replies “It was in the pub, if my poor memory is to be trusted, but you know how bad I am about those things” She smiled. “Anyway, I am glad to find you well, especially you Tom. Forgive an old woman’s foolish worry. Let us help you so we may finish this nasty business.”

I had been watching Zunk the whole time and so saw him tense in the most imperceptible way. But I had seen this before recently, many times, and it generally ended with flashing steely knives. Whatever was going on (and I confess I did not know anything certain), Zunk was not liking it and was about to take matters into their own hands.

“Our first drink was in Ordwii’s kitchen and it was double distilled rum. You would know that if you were her, so who the hell are you?” His voice had risen to a shout by this time, matching the rise of his bow. They fired at Ordwii, or whoever it was.

The arrow never came close, deflecting off some invisible force surrounding NotOrdwii, and she smiled. It was feral and bloodthirsty. Definitely not Ordwii. “That was both rude and foolish, little goblin” her voice low and deadly. Never had Ordwii ever spoken such. “Time to teach you cubs a hard lesson”. NotOrdwii snapped her fingers.

A bloom of energy surrounded us, and we became slow and clumsy. As we floundered at our weapons, NotOrdwii conjured a huge ball of flame that she hurled toward us, detonating it above our heads. I closed my eyes, whispering my fealty to Milani Moonglow, even as I was about to be incinerated by the heat of the sun, only my flesh did not sear and char as I expected. There was a boom, and a flash, and stunning pain unlike anything I have known, but no red hot death. I blacked out.

I awoke, bound by both magic and rope, propped up on a stone block, the others all around me. That is, except for Tom, who was stretched out on a big stone slab, rivulets of blood pooling on the floor. He was not dead, but he would not last long either. Beside him was the headless corpse of Ragnulf, his blood mingling with Tom’s and draining into a channel that ended at the altar with the shimmering portal. It seemed larger and the shadow figures clearer. This was beyond anything we could ever hope to stop; my apologies to Milani were as useless as I was.

“Ahh” purred NotOrdwii “you are awake”. Her voice was low, throaty and self-satisfied. She did not look like Ordwii anymore, and though my head was still foggy, I thought she resembled the red armor clad figure in the murals. Sure enough, Hans confirmed this by saying “Belimarius, what are you doing here? This cannot end any better for you than it did for Kharzoug, your time is past. You should cease this madness”

“Ah” she replied “At least one of you is smart enough to recognize me. Well done, little mage. But you are wrong. Kharzoug’s death opened a path for me, and it is most ironic that those responsible for his death should also further my ascendance. I could not believe my good luck when Elsie revealed to me that this one <pointing at the semi-conscious Tom> is blood heir to one of those who defeated Kharzoug. So delicious, no? The blood of the traitor <points at Ragnulf> and the blood of the hero together will open the way. At least this way the imbecile will accomplish something through his death. I don’t actually know if this is part of the Red Bishop’s plan, but I care not, nor do I care if I have changed whatever fate he had in mind for you”.

“What are you talking about, witch?” I shouted. ”We have nothing do with this, or you, or any Red Bishop.”

Zunk was struggling against his bonds the whole time, becoming more frantic. “Unbind me you fucking bitch” they growled, in a voice so unlike Zunk’s that it made my skin crawl. “I swear I will gut you like a fish and slice that grin off your face if you do not untie me”

Hans was more diplomatic. “Just let us go, we have no part to play here. Tom has already done his. We will take him and go, and leave you to whatever plans you have. We can hardly stop you.”

“Oh, you are quite right, but also quite wrong, little mage. You cannot stop me. But you also have some role to play, although I do not know what it is. At the least, you will bear witness to my victory. Do you think it is chance that brought you here? The Red Bishop’s hand is in all of this.”

“Take you, priest. Your shipwreck was not an accident, and your mother is not dead, but on the mainland. And you, feral thief, did you never wonder about why your father left Thistletop and estranged himself from your kin? What actually caused the explosion that brought the house down on your luckless father, little mage? Do you really believe he was that dimwitted? You have no idea how you have been led.”

“Lies” I shouted. You..fucking..witch! Whatever purpose you have in tormenting us with these tales, I will not submit to your deception, much as I would like to. I will not comply with this fiction. And…your armor looks..like shit.” I would have stomped my foot if I could.

“Brave words, priest. Stupid but brave. What will you do when your friend bleeds out in front of you? Ah, I see the imminent opening has attracted some friends” Huge demons, almost too terrible to look at, began appearing. I stopped looking at 3-we could not defeat even one, even assuming we were unbound and at full strength.

I looked at Tom, pale as a ghost, eyes barely focused staring at the bitch in front of him. “I am sorry, Thomas, for not being stronger and wiser. May we meet again in Milani’s light”

If he could hear my whispered words, I could not tell. But he..sneezed? Of all the reactions-this was not one of them I expected. A shrug, I could see, or a smile of defiance, but this? It might have been my imagination, but he seemed to focus at a point behind Belimarius.

Suddenly something warped in the space Tom peered at. It was black, and gleaming. There were silvery glints and flashes of dull orange. Movements too fast for me to see. I had no idea what I was looking at.

But whatever it was, Belimarius was taking furious wounds. The silver flashed on kidney, chest, hamstring and throat, each time opening a gash that poured out inky black smoke. “Gods damn you, you fucking alley cat!” Belimarius shrieked “How many times before you learn you cannot defeat me”?

“Tsk tsk” came the thick accented words “How many times before you realize we will find you wherever you go, and I will send you back until you stop” A double strike of blades across the neck that released another stream of black smoke, and the figure collapsed into itself like a spent balloon and was gone, her last words echoing “I WILL be back”.

“What a bitch” muttered the black clad figure “She has as many lives as me, if I am counting right”

Suddenly it clicked, and had I not been bound, I might have keeled over.

Orange cat folk. Flashing knives too fast to follow. Eastern accent. Cocksure beyond belief.

If the devil himself had appeared before me and challenged me to a bet, I would have taken it in an instant. This was Promqeth of the Pride of the Irritated Kitty, protector of Saandpoint, and with the other heroes, dragon slayer, killer of Kharzoug. Here. Fish and fiddlesticks and what the hell?

As if in confirmation, two more figures appear in a pop, one tall and gaunt with an air of oracular power almost too intense to bear, holding a polearm of gleaming black metal, dancing with energy. The other smallish, sharp snouted and bewhiskered, about the size of Hans, holding a staff twice as tall as it, in robes with swirling runes that made my eyes hurt to look at.

“So yeah, Vistaro-check those fucking demons while I shut down this portal. You know what time it is right? It’s fireball time!” The voice was nasal and kind of crude, and none of the stories Da read to me included the profanity, but there was no doubt who it was: Stan of the Flaming Sphere.

Vistaro gestured, a casual flick, and walls of gleaming blades surrounded the demons, holding them in place. It made Meitremar’s cloud look like the party trick of a 3rd rate hedge wizard. The demons howled in frustration in their bladed prison as massive balls of flame crashed against the walls.

“Fucking fuck!” yelled Stan “Kharzoug’s hairy forked cock! There’s some sort of entropic disruption field coupled to the gate, and it’s gonna take me too fucking long to figure out the right harmonics to take it down. What the fuck?”

A fourth figure had appeared as all this was playing out. Taller even then Vistaro, and broad shouldered, holding an immense blade, implacable calm in a raging storm. I could sense the divinity surrounding him and wondered what it must be like to be held that closely to a god. It was Viktor Fidelus, known by Tom, as Dad. And Known by others as Viktor of Iomidae, or Viktor the Lich Killer (but this last was only whispered in panic in dark halls).

“Stan” says Viktor “Buy us what time you can. I must attend to my son, and we must give these brave younglings what advice we can. Move! Time is wasting; my son is dying”.

“Yeah yeah. Keep your shirt on big guy. I got ya.” Stan made a complex motion of his hands, and suddenly we were inside a shell of multicolored light.

Viktor approached his son and laid his hands upon him. “I am so sorry this has happened, my boy. I thought I could protect you by not entangling you in my affairs, but there are other forces at play I did not recognize. I should have listened more to Vistaro’s auguries.” As he spoke, light suffused from Viktor’s hands, instantly healing all the damage and returning Tom to full strength. I could not imagine the amount of energy that took, but it was nothing to Viktor, whose face betrayed no strain, and was full only of concern for his son.

Vistaro snapped his fingers again, and we were freed from our bonds, magical and otherwise. “Arise, brave ones, your friend is healed, but we do not have much time. Forces are gathering”

“We should port the fuck outta here is what we should be doing” offers Stan. “Live to fight another day, and all that bullshit, amiright??”

“I do not think so Stan” returns Vistaro. “Look at the portal. Look at the shapes within-do you recall the last time we were in such a position?”.

“Toureg’s big balls” snapped Stan “. Like I could forget. You and that idiot Javis jumped through to take down the portal to Leng, and fluff ball over here couldn’t stand the thought of missing out so he jumped in too. You were gone for years while I was stuck here figuring out how to get your asses back. Tell me you ain’t thinking of doing this again?”

“Stan-Vistaro is right” says Promqeth. “Portal cannot be closed from this side. Only other. We must go in to buy these ones time, as Vistaro has said-they have some part to play. Ours is there” as he points towards the shimmering gate. “All of us, this time.”

“ahhh fuck, yeah. Okay. Whatever. What do you want?” This last was directed towards Hans, who had, somewhat boldly, approached Stan. “Jah, well, if you wouldn’t mind before you go, I’d like to ask you some questions of the arcane nature to aid in my research, which maybe you know is…”

“Yeah yeah, I get it” says Stan, brusquely but not unkindly. “There’s stuff you need to know etcetera. Listen kid, you remind me of me. Ya got potential. But you’re not ready for it now, although I’d bet you will be. So, uhh, here take this instead, it’ll help more.Keep yer wits about you-dance with what brung you-don’t overthink it. See ya when I see ya”. Hans nodded, and mutely took the offered wand, turning it this way and that, trying to divine its nature, already lost in the mystery.

As this was unfolding, Vistaro approached me, his deep gaze taking me in and making me quite uncomfortable. I shifted from foot to foot and smiled. “Uhhh, thank you for unbinding us” I offered lamely. Was that the best I could do? Stupid girl.

“The road ahead of you will be hard” he says, not unkindly. “You will be required to do many things to keep your comrades safe, too many, if my experience is any guide. It will test you and you might fail-probably you will on occasion. <His eyes were clearly somewhere else, fr a moment>. But have faith in your god, and yourself, you are stronger than you think. Here, take this with my blessing to protect those around you”

I could only stand hands out as he placed a gleaming wooden staff on my outstretched palms. It was simply and elegantly carved, and warm to the touch. “Thank you greatly” I managed eventually. “I will remember your words and do my best, and even if I fail, I will never give up. Thank you” and I bowed my head.

Promqeth was kneeling by Zunk, who had a look on his face I had never seen before. A mixture of admiration, fascination, attraction and a little fear. He shivered slightly as the cat gently took their wrists, examining the burns. “Such as us do not like to be bound, do we?” Zunk stared at the great green eyes regarding him. “A little advice, friend. You are a rogue, act like one. Trust your companions to create opportunity; you are a knife in the dark, the unseen dirk that strikes deep and quick. The open hand above hiding fatal blow from below. Be clever. Here, I have carried this blade for a long time; it came from a place of bitterness, and I give it to you to reclaim it. Do not disappoint me, young thief.”

Zunk took the proffered blade “We will meet again, Promqeth” he says, his voice thick “And you will know me”.

Viktor looks about him. “Who would have thought you were all so sentimental?” He looks at Tom, whose face is unreadable. “Thank you for the boons, Father. And for saving me and my friends. Do you really have to go, again? Can’t the others do it?”

Viktors calm shatters and he places his hands on Tom’s shoulders. “I wish this to be so son, you have no idea. Would that I could have been closer to you growing up, but too many would seek their revenge on me by hurting you, and being apart was better than you being dead. Even so, it has come to naught, you have been dragged into this now, and we must part for a time, each to our own path. There will not be a day when I do not think on you, and I know you will make me proud.”

Viktor places an amulet around Tom’s neck. “This will help protect you till we meet again, son. I look forward to your tales of bravery and kinship.”

Viktor’s face is a mask of sadness, Tom’s stoic as usual. I cannot bear it. “For Milani’s sakes” I blurt out “give him a hug, Thomas and let him leave with your love in his heart”.

He does. Of all the healing I have ever done, this might be my finest.

“Hey, hate to break up the family reunion, but this sphere ain’t gonna last forever. If we’re gonna go, we fucking need to go. Hey frog boy-you need to stay here and get these others out-get em on the boat to sandpoint. Those demons are gonna be slashed from the blade wall and spitting mad, so arrow up motherfucker. You’re almost as good as I am with this plane shit as I am now. So keep that bulging eye out for us, okay?”

A figure I had not seen before came from behind one of the larger stones. “Bwaaark. Okey dokey. Go and create chaos, I’ll take here of things here” and Timrik the Summoner licked his eyeball. Okey dokey?

The shimmering globe was paler now, and the bloody demons howled from the other side as the 4 heroes surrounded the altar. “Do not look so glum, Stan” says Promqeth “You missed last one so is good you get to go now, and Viktor too. We are together-what could go wrong?”

“Well” says Vistaro “quite a lot actually, starting with… “He trails off as he looks around, his gaze settling on Viktor and then Tom. “Yes, yes, I am sure it will be fine. On the count of three.” And then they were gone, to wherever.

“Bwaaarp. The sphere is about to go, so be ready to run when I say so. Live to fight another day so you may partake of the great end of days! Find Javis at the harbor; he will take you where you need to go. And step back a bit, we will need some room” We? Just as I was wondering about Timrik’s mental state, he began speaking words in the most guttural tongue I have ever heard and 3 lion things appeared flanking him. If lions were the size of a small elephant, and as imagined by some far eastern painter. They roared their challenge at the demons, who roared back in equal ferocity. Timrik sent a flight (3 arrows at once) that managed somehow to penetrate the swirling wall of blades, piercing a demon in each eye, and the throat. It staggered back and was shredded by the blades, which then disappeared letting the other demons advance.

“You should thank me demon-spawn; your deaths will help pave the way for the final reckoning. An honor it is” and then he turned to us “RUN”. And we did, not looking back until we reached the harbor, where a low slung black sloop awaited us, with a hooded figure standing on the prow.

“Come” says Javis “before any more mischief can occur. I will explain on the way”
