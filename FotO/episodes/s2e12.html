Season 2, Episode 12: The Call of Sisters and of Fathers

There didn’t seem to be much more to say to Da, and he didn’t seem to want hear more either. He took his leave, sort of stiffly, making a point to nod to Tom and Zunk. They responded politely in kind.

“Thanks” I said, hugging them both “my Da’s just scared, but he means well. I guess we need to go back to the Dragon so you can meet your mom, Zunk, and I have to meet the women at Hannah’s. Maybe Hans will have sobered up by now”

We arrived at Hans’ room to learn he was less drunk, if not quite completely sober, and a little testy. He kept telling me to stop shouting. I guess I was still a little mad (okay, more than a little).

“Why do you care what these people think of you anyway, Jhyselein?”

Well, that was a good question. And I realized I was not really mad because I was being dragged through the mud-Hans was right. It was more that I was furious about how people (men) needed to scold and judge us (women) so badly that they make stuff up in order to do so, and people like Hans’ mom let that happen.

“Hans, I know it wasn’t exactly your fault. You can’t help that men are pigs, or that your Mom is clueless. But Hans, at least try to see what is going on here! Why is it always that men are trying to tear women down because of who they share their bed with? I have stuff to do-good luck with Zunk and his Mom.”

“Jah, Ok Jhys, but don’t slam the door, ok?. My head is..”

I pretended not to hear as I left, slamming the door behind me. It was mean, if I am being honest, but I was tired of being told what to do. I went to Hannah’s shop and left the others to their rendezvous. I only found out what happened later, and here is what I learned:

What I learned is that Zunk has a complicated life. I guess that shouldn’t be surprising given his profession. I never really thought about what their life was like before we met; I just accepted they were Ordwii’s friend. I might have to be more careful about that-not that I regret anything; Zunk has a good heart regardless, and many a rogue has served justice, like Promqeth. But still, I might not always be so lucky-rule #2 (things are not what the seem).

Anyway, it turns out Zunk stole something from someone powerful, for someone else powerful and it went bad, somehow. Zunk was light on the details, but it was clear something happened between the time he took what he was directed to and when the deal was done. And their sibling might be involved. So Zunk had to make himself scarce, and quickly, and a series of unfortunate decisions, or maybe-the lack of good alternatives, led him to stow away on a ship that sunk outside Osprey, where Ordwii took them in.

But Zunk’s performance in court reached Venke, who is clearly connected and familiar with the network Scarnazzi is plugged into. So when they were leaned on to accept Scarnazzi’s contract, Venke counseled her husband to agree, mainly to keep an eye on Zunk.

“She was kind of mad at me, to be honest, for standing up in court and making myself known, but what was I going to do? I couldn’t let Scarnazzis do the town dirty like that”. (This is why I trust Zunk) “But I know she isn’t going to let Titus have his way. She’ll slow walk it as much as she can”

Zunk felt sure this would work, especially if we could continue to be a distraction, which well, doesn’t seem to be a problem. There’s a young girl with a broken cheekbone, and I need to deliver a message to the Scarnazzis on her behalf. I plan to be quite distracting.

Oh-but I haven’t gotten to that part, yet.

As Junk and the others were meeting at the Dragon, I was at Hannah’s herbalist shop. Given all the sex-shaming, I had expected to be gently offered remedies for pregnancy termination or disease, or god forbid, male performance enhancements (so so gross). Boy was I wrong.

Hannah’s shop was lit, light showing behind the drawn curtains, and I knocked on the door. I was welcomed in by a ½ elf woman in her middle years, who introduced herself as Hannah. “B’ethana you know, this is Brin (a powerful dark skinned woman ressed in working leather), who runs the smithy with her husband. That’s Kaye and one of her girls, Lila. Kaye runs the Kitten”.

I didn’t quite know what to make of this gathering, although I’m not sure what I expected. It seemed an unusual group, “Hi” I greeted the room as I entered “So what’s this about?

Kaye and Lila looked up from their conversation, and I sucked in my breath, hard. One side of Lila’s face was a mass of purple and black bruises, and her eye was partially swollen shut.

Oh Milani’s grace, what’s happened here!? Are you alright? Can I help? Let me take a look. Is that Ok, Lira”?

“Ta ma’am its fine “(Ma’am? I was a year older, at best). “Sad to say I’ve seen worse, haven’t I?” she continued, rather chirpily under the present circumstances “Miss Kaye and Miss Hannah got me healin’ good. Don’t hurt but a bit now.”

Kaye stood up and greeted me, taking my hands. “Your offer is greatly appreciated, but not necessary. And not why we brought you here. Lila-why don’t you tell Jhyselein what happened?”

“So it was a couple or 3 days ago when I was working tables at the Kitten, like usual. Some o’ them Snotty Boys, as we call ‘em, piled in. One ‘o them’s a mean snake, Gervis-that they call Iron Jaw. He, uh, (and she looked around suddenly, like she was about to run, until Kaye took her hand) and, he, uh caught me outside on my shift change and well, you see” She was quiet for a moment and looked down.

“Why would he do that?” I cried.

Lila looked up, clear eye hard, and shrugged. “don’t need no reason, do they? Because they can. Snotty Boys think they own this town, all of us.”

“This is…monstrous, wicked” I grasped for words, I was getting mad again. “I will put a stop to this, as Milani’s spirit is my witness.”

“I, we” Kaye corrected herself “know you will if given the chance. But there’s more you need to hear. Finish your story, Lira”

“Well, I got away didn’t I? Lila said, with just a little smile “He took a swing at me and caught his foot so I scrambled away and hid down ‘neath the docks. That’s where I found it. I just looked and it were there, like it had been waiting” It would have made little sense except for the fact Lila was pointing at a big wooden box on the table, sea swollen and barnacle encrusted.

B’ethana reached inside and pulled out a flat carved stone tablet a couple of handspans square. It was a relief of a many-armed winged woman, portrayed weirdly flat, bordered by runes of a type I have never seen.

I looked up wordlessly at them. I had no idea what was going on.

Hannah spoke. “We think this is a reference to an old story, one that hardly any now recall, about a dangerous beast that was locked away for the protection of good folk”

I nodded, wondering where this was going.

“But some of us recall this story had an even less talked of caution, which was to never forget the story is told by the winners”

“I see” I said slowly (I did not) “but why bring this to me, and not Brodert, or Abstalar? Not that I won’t try, but..”

“We didn’t bring this to the others because “ Hannah started.

“They are men” Brin finished.

Hannah sighed and Kaye snorted. “Brin’s not wrong” Kaye offered, but it’s not the whole of it, either. We brought it to you because you see us, they don’t. Mostly because they are men, and men are mostly in charge, and most people in charge see only what they want to see. You see the people who get ground down, and you speak for us. To us. We heard what you said in court, and in the Dragon. Something about this (gesturing at the stone) needs to be seen and heard too and you’re the one we trust to do it”.

“Okay” I said gravely. “I will try to honor this, but there is something you can do too. You want understanding, then perhaps the best place to start is to give it. Nualia’s story is not so different from the one you are telling me here. Yes, she did terrible things, which she is owning. But do you not see how the harm that had been done to her is swept aside when people tell Nuali’s tale? Do we need to wait 1000 years to correct her story”

“Sorry” I shrugged. “I just feel sad for her. She’s suffered enough. Anyway, that’s for later. Let me take a closer look at this stone”

I reached for the stone and everything suddenly went pitch black. I saw feral serpent eyes glow in front of me and felt something grip my wrist. I looked down and saw it was clasped by long dark talons. How I could see this with no light, I had no idea. “Run” said a voice in my mind “your friends are in danger”. The voice was insistent, urgent and convincing. light came on and I ran, and I don’t remember what I said (or didn’t) on the way out.

I was out the door and into the street before I realized I had no idea where I was going; the voice was impossible to deny and filled my head. Then it didn’t matter, because I somehow actually did know where to go, and before too long I found myself underneath the docks, maybe the very place Lila had hidden. I saw my friends in a loose cluster, and a man-ish thing threatening Hans, who looked quite bloody. Other things were shambling out of the dark water.

Hans took another hit and needed aid. Close combat was not his thing, so I healed him as soon as I was near enough and then tried to get between him and the pale, stiffly shambling man-thing. A trio of others were getting closer-Zunk arrowed one while Tom struck at another. He was using his shield to good effect, blunting partially some of man-things. Blows. They are dressed in common clothes, and looked briny, but they were feral and their movements somehow wrong.

I stepped up and channeled Milani’s spirit to help my friends strike true, and was wounded. A figure in the back I barely saw raised his hands and we are struck by shadows that hurt even more, and which harmed the man-things as well. Hans has gotten out of the fray and conjured a blast of water that knocked one down and Tom severed it in two, and a large utterly disgusting white worm slithers onto the ground twitching and is still. So so gross.

I channeled darts of energy at the thing in front of me, infusing it with cold iron essence on a whim. The effect was spectacular-the thing was greatly scored by my conjuration, and Hans sent cold iron tinged arcane bolts at it that punch in to the wounds I had made, sending the thing down.

I noticed a whisp of smoke way in the back, that rose out of sight as the man figure collapsed into the water, but I didn’t have time to think too much about it. Tom was bleeding from numerous wounds, so I used Milani’s energy to make him whole, just as the man-thing split from the chest down and the ugly worm inside broke out, wounds closing as it absorbed the manshell it had worn. It chomps on Tom, who slashed with his sword. Then Zunk appeared, blades slashing in parallel flat arcs that takes the worms head off. Tom blocked the blow from the one behind that also shed its skin, and a flight of bolts from Hans thunked wetly into worm flesh. Zunk took a sliding step past it, opening up a wound down most of its body. The blunty jawed head reared up and then slammed onto the ground.

“Fish and Fiddlesticks” I yelled “what the heck happened here?” Hans, you’re still bleeding-Phryllyis-will you get out of there! I need to see what’s going on”

“Okay Okay-I just don’t like worms is all”

“Tom” I said as I worked on Hans, “I’ll tend to you in a moment-meantime-what just happened? Weren’t you to meet with Zunks mom?”

Tom looked around at the corpses, the worms leaking gloopy milky fluid onto the sand, and shrugged. “Yeah, I guess it got a little weird there, huh? Anyway, we talked to Venke-she wanted somewhere more private than the Dragon, cause that seemed too, dangerous” And he grinned. “So we came here. As she was leaving, that guy from the morning-you know with the burn marks, who fed the birds? I think he said his name was Billy. He recognized me, you know? Like who my father was.”

Billy? Burn Marks?

“Holy smokes, Tom. Of course he recognized you-that Billy knew your Dad. Prom kept his eye on the lad, and they saved him when the dragon attacked. That must be where he got those burns!”

“Well, he had this too” as he held out a ring “He kept saying it was an anchor, or a key, and something about a portal. I think Viktor and the rest might need our help. Anyway, when I took the ring there was this weird thing that happened and then those guys came out of the water and then you showed up.”

I was mending Tom by this point, and Zunk and Hans had gone out to the body in the sea, having discovered nothing of interest , but definitely much grossness, about the worm-men-things. They looked like street toughs and dockies, but taken over by some awful evil parasite.

Tom and I joined the other two looking at the body in the surf. It was completely whole and the cause of death was not obvious. I did a quick pass and felt something nasty, so I probed further.

“Oh my” I murmured “This can’t be good. This man was possessed by an Invictus demon”

“Jah?” Hans said “I don’t know that one-can you be more specific perhaps, please?

“Terrible things capable of possession and complete control” I replied “They say it’s impossible to detect-the only way I could tell now was because it sacrificed its host, and that leaves a stain. I have no idea how to detect possession”

“So it could be anyone” said Zunk. Not a question. I nodded. “Aye, that’s not good” I nodded again.

“Look, I said “We got problems. The Scarnazzis are worse than we thought, and we got to do something, and there’s this other thing too that I learned that I have to tell you. I don’t have any idea how this fits in. How can things be so tangled up” I stamped my foot. “Anyway, lets get out of here-it’s gross. I need a drink. I think I saw a Lamia.”

I hadn’t actually been thinking that at all until I said it, but it seemed right. Those burning eyes when I was warned of danger? I had seen those, or something very like them, in a picture in A Kitty’s Tale, when The Pride first came upon Xaneesha, the Lamia Matriarch who was Prom’s mentor/tormentor/ally. I didn’t think it was Xaneesha (did I?), but maybe her cousin?

Her..I thought of the stone tablet and shook my head. Some things are too hard to believe, even now.
