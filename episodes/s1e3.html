Season 1, Episode 3: Fish and Fiddlesticks

I grabbed Zunk and raised them up to climb again and whispered to Hans.” Grab hold, we can do this together.” I hoped he wasn’t offended, but the little guy just wasn’t going to make it on his own. I climbed up imagining it was fore-mast rigging and not some rope heading me into certain death. Okey dokey-that sort of worked,

I popped my head up to see a guard in janky rusted armor eyeing me. “Hey-you’re not supposed to be here. Willy-get Steve, I’m gonna sort this” and he stomps towards me with his hand on his weapon, some sort of sickle. Well, maybe I can talk my way out if this. I hope Hans has climbed down and is out of sight. Don’t see Zunk either, but out of the corner of my eye I catch Tom crouched along the wall, out of the guard’s vision.

“Uh..hi? I’m looking for my friend Orwii? She came in earlier with Vanda and hasn’t met us like we agreed so I’m sort of worried. Uhh, sorry to be barging in like this but there was nobody at the gate and so, well.. <smile>

He came at me swinging. Fish sticks..this place is SO predictable! I was just about to raise my shield when Tom lunges out of hiding with his long blade and..Cuts.The. Guy. In. Half. It was sooo disgusting. Sprayed blood all over! That was definitely not predictable.

I wasn’t going to stand there in a pool of blood gawping like a girl, so I chased after the other guard who shoved open the door and ran into the building. I turned the corner and, holy smokes, a couple of other dead guys on the ground and more blood everywhere! What did these guys do? Well, I was just tired of blood and rust, so I screamed something like “You terrible people, what is with you?” and I swung my own weapon in fury, catching him full on. He swipes me with the sickle pretty hard, and trips me to boot (I hate these people) but I roll back up and smite him again, this time for good. Huh. I’m sorry he’s dead, but he didn’t exactly not deserve it.

Hans inspects the bodies while I just sort of stare at the one I killed. The ones on the floor look like they died of a bunch of spear wounds, holes everywhere. Rusty armor like the ones we killed-what is going on here? We’re in a cramped boxy room, the walls to each side with arrow slits and murder holes-if there were people behind them, we’d be dead. Maybe that explains the spear wounds. Hans is fussing around in the corner with something and there is loud click and then an..ahhh…salty cod! that hurts. I look down at the spear wound on my leg. A little to the left and I’d be skewered. Ah, well it’s not all bad news! At least we figured out what got the guys lying on the floor.

“Uhhhh sorry” says Hans. “I was just examining this discrepancy in the stone work here and oh, oh, are you are right”? Well yes, although I have to call on Milani to grant me a boon. “Yes, I’m OK, except for these little flakes of rust” I say. Something to worry about later.

“Aye there mate” offers Zunk, “maybe leave the investigating to me next time. It’s sort of my thing, so you’re gonna have to trust me to tell you what’s safe and what ain’t”

There appears to be nobody around as we search the rooms. There’s a sort of clinic and I recognize some healing draughts, and there is a very nice kit, even better than the one I have, which should come in handy. There’s a set of stairs down, and we recall Daril telling us that’s where he’d hide prisoners in such a place like this so I insist we go that way. It’s dark and dusty with stacked barrels of ale or such, and little else. A couple of lazy cats tired of catching rats is the only thing interest-I’ve always liked cats. There’s door with a lock Zunk can’t sort, and a big hole in one of the walls-a tunnel that heads off beyond what we can see, with some blood smears right inside. I wonder if Ordwii is down that way (and Cormac too, I feel bad forgetting about that little guy), but no, it would be just like things here if she were somewhere upstairs while we crawl around in the dark. Stupid.

We go back upstairs, again finding nothing but rusted weapons and mail, although there is a bronze talisman of some sort that Hans said adds tricks to your armor. The privy that Hans insisted on inspecting was lovely. Better/worse was nasty kitchen (unless I have the two reversed). Opening another door, we are faced by a shabby but well-muscled man who cries out as if mortally threated, yelling he’ll take as many Gorumites out as he can before he dies and then strikes at Tom (who has politely taken the lead for all his eyerolling) with a blacksmith’s hammer. I half expected Tom to run him through like he did the last guard (well, okay, that’s not true. I was absolutely fully expecting to be splattered with blood), but instead Tom trips him, and calls for the fighting to stop. Zunk says “ Hey mate, we gotcha. We ain’t no friends of them Gorumites either and they ain’t lookin so good. Whole bunch of ‘em dead outside. We mean you no harm, mate. Can ya tell us how you got here”?

“Ja ja” Hans is nodding and I am smiling as best I can while being all splattered with blood. Hope not too much of it is showing in the dim light-the man looks really rattled. He warily drops his hammer a little bit and says “Well what the fuck you are you doing here then, you fools?” He nods from time to time as we explain, starting from the rusting dead body and ending with our killing the guards downstairs. When we are done, his hammer is fully lowered and he looks thoughtful.

“Hmmph. I am Trygve, and I should probably tell you my story. “

“A week ago, a Thassilonian man named Meitremar arrived, claiming to be a merchant from Xin-Edasseril. He spoke with Lord Ragnulf for an entire night. When they emerged at dawn, they gathered us all in the worship hall and spoke of bringing war to Osprey Cove, subjugating all other settlements on Chakikoth Isle, and forging an alliance with Xin-Edasseril.

“At the end, Lord Ragnulf spoke of a sickness among our ranks and claimed that his new friend Meitremar had brought him a way to expose that sickness. He opened a bottle, and it filled the hall with foul-smelling air. Several of my kin doubled over in pain—it looked as if their skin was rusting—and Lord Ragnulf ordered the rest of us to ‘put down the blasphemers’. I’m glad to say I did not mindlessly obey, but many others did, and the fighting that followed was awful.

“I alone survived. They keep me alive because they value my skill at working metal, but they no longer worship the Lord in Iron. They see glory in the rust instead. Meitremar and Lord Ragnulf have gone beyond the rusted door below now, and closed it tight in their wake, but they left behind that awful, split-faced monster to keep those who have betrayed my kin in line. You must save Lord Ragnulf! He knows not what he’s done!”

I was so mad by the end of this I could hardly speak. Why would anyone here cut a deal with a fat bloodsucking merchant from across the sea to make war on their own neighbors? And why are evil people so stupid? Like the first thing in their grand conquest is to poison their own men and release a terrible plague in the very spot they will conquer? Does that make any sense at all? We have to stop this, even If Ordwii and Cormac were not involved. Tryg thinks they have been taken as part of the plan.

I ask if he thinks the door we found in the basement is where they have gone, and he says no-that door is far away. But he does happen to have a key to the door in the basement, which is really helpful. We give him some water and rations so he can get out while we go down and open that door.

We brushed the curious cats out of the way, and Zunk opened the door with Tryg’s key. The smell was appalling-like the privy but with overtones of human sickness and misery. Three scrawny and weak acolytes were lying amidst filth and whatnot, everything stained with excrement. Help us-one croaked weakly.

One of the kitties brushed my leg and I bent down to gently move it aside, and ohmygodthatisnota kitty whatthehellis that thing? Thing, which is suddenly is large dog sized has a scrunched up face with large teeth, big claws and a lizardy tail lunges up…and I am suddenly on my back side while Zunk is pouring something down my throat. Thing’s wretched slavering jaws are the only thing I see, wait..that was before.

“Feeling better?” they say “wouldn’t want my carryall to come to harm, eh”.

I roll up, fending off more blows and mash it hard. Bad kitty! A blur rushes by me and the other thing is now menacing Tom who slices it in two. More blood everywhere, again. (He really can be a most violent teen). I whack the ugly beast in front of me again and Tom finishes it off.

Okey dokey. Even the cats are out to get us, I guess. Good to know.

We minister to the acolytes with food and water. They can barely speak for their ills, but we manage to learn they have been imprisoned since the kinslaughter, locked in here without food or drink, clearly left to die. Their lord has been bewitched by the Thallasonian merchant and forsaken Gorum for someone or something they called Zar Azmak, and they all have fled beyond the rusty door, whereever it is. Ah..oh…I look to the tunnel.

It is all a little confusing but adds slightly to Tryg’s tale. If I had to guess, I’d say that Zar Azmak is Meitremar’s godly patron and the merchant is entreating it to help in his conquest. Such godly boons come at a cost, often paid in blood, so that they have taken our friends is even more alarming. Much as I would like to chase Meitremar and Ragnulf and find Ordwii and Cormac, there might be people still upstairs, in danger from the splitface thing. So first things first. We escort the acolytes to the front gate. They are weak, but can make it to Elsie’s, where I am sure they will find care.

Back upstairs and upstairs again to the top floor, which must be living quarters. We search through and opening another door at the far end, we see two more berusted guards and Vanda, magnificent in her bright not rusted armor.

“Well” she says to the guards, “what are you waiting for?” Not with rancor mind you, but they still attack

One steps up and delivers a terrible blow to Tom, who is nearly taken off his feet. Somehow, he rallies and runs his man through, but Tom is nearly done. No!. I will not let this happen! and I channel Milani’s gift, cleansing Tom of most of his wounds, and backhand the other guard who has moved into the door. Hans fries the guard and Vanda, who gives us a frown ” “I wish you hadn’t done that” says she and just as I think she will reach for her great sword, she sits on the edge of the bed.

“Hey” shouts the guard “I could use a little help here”

“You’re doing fine” was the return.

Tom steps forward and finishes the job, sending the other guard to whatever awaits him in the afterlife. Vanda and the dead guard must have differed on the meaning of “fine”.

We regard Vanda, who is still sitting on the bed, head down. “I have made a grave error” she quietly says “and betrayed everything I stand for. I..let him send those men to their deaths while he and that cursed Meitremar plotted the sacking of Iron Harbor, and your village Osprey too. I stood by as they took your friends and fled to Rusthenge, where they no doubt have some vile act planned. I have nothing left but bitterness and rust.”

I could not bear to see here like this. It was worse than her raising her sword against us.

“No” I urged. “you were confused, and Ragnulf was your lord. Yes, you did a terrible thing but it also matters what you do next. Come with us and we will handle them together. I know you have this in you.”

She considers this, but she doesn’t trust herself or her judgment. “No, I leave it you to find your friend and avenge the deaths of these men for me. If I am to wash away this sin, I must find another way.”

Sigh. “okay “I say ”We will do our best, but first we have to find some split faced thing that has terrorized everyone. Do you know this creature?”

She perks up. “Ohh “ she growls “That one I’d absolutely like to kill. I was not sure that I could take it on my own, but with your help I am willing to try. It’s in the room beyond. Our best chance is for me to go in one door while you go in the other and surround it. What say you”?

You betcha.

We head to the north door and Vanda the south, and charge in as agreed. The creature hisses and spits and raises claws. The origin of the name is clear-the thing’s mouth is twisted sideways giving the illusion of a two faced being. The teeth look fully functional, sadly.

The monster and Vanda must have some sort of history; it springs towards her and delivers a blow of such force I am not sure Tom would have survived, and it nearly does her in. In desperation, I call upon Milani to grant me power, and my weapon glows with godly energy. Tom moves into to engage and seems suddenly beset by woe, holding his hands to his face as if trying to hide from the world, all vigor gone from his hunched form. Vanda strikes the monster full on with her great sword, wounding it greatly, but it rakes her with it’s claws again and now she is done, collapsing to the ground.

Fish and fiddlesticks! I have to do something, and the cry “For Vanda” is in my throat as I raise my glowing weapon. Zunk rushes by me just as I am about to charge, and strikes with their clever knives, into the gaping wound from Vanda’s sword. The horrible thing collapses with a gurgling moan, and I bestow Milani’s grace upon Vanda just in time.

Tom recovers after a moment or two. “Whoa” he says “That was even more depressing than normal”
