Season 1, Episode 8: Boggards Point the Way

Zunk ran up to Ordwii and they embraced warmly, while I smiled, hopefully not too nervously. “I am glad my trust in you to keep them well was not misplaced, Jhyseline” I beamed. “And I am sure you looked after them in your clever manner too, Hans. I have no doubt you found my letter directing you here.” Oops. I looked at Hans and we both let the matter slide.

“Anyway” Ordwii continued “You best explain how you have come to be here. And have you found Cormac?, he was injured gravely while protecting me, by him.” She glared at Ragnulf.

She was somewhat mollified by learning Cormac was safe, and she let be the matter of Ragnulf’s presence. “I have little to add unfortunately, being kept here for these last days, and am afraid I will be of little help; they have taken my holy symbols so I am without my god. It is best if I return to Iron Harbor-you have convinced me all is not lost there and so I will help them prepare for what lies ahead. But you must grant me one last bit of aid and accompany me there-I am weak and was unconscious on the way in. I cannot find my way out on my own”.

It seemed the best course of action-even Hans could not think of a good reason for her to stay with us, so we retraced our steps back up the long tunnel and then to Elsies.

We had forgotten one complication; Ragnulf. The townspeople were no more happy to see him then Ordwii (or Cormac for that matter) and were fractious. They had to be convinced not to hang the dwarf immediately; Zunk managed to talk them down and they agreed on a proper trial to judge the dwaf’s deeds. Ragnulf was silent and resigned as his life was bargained for, but when it was decided, he said “Although I do not deserve it, I ask to be allowed the manner of my own death. if I be found guilty. I will walk into the sea, fully armored and take my final rest there.” Muttering, but granted.

Tere wasn’t much to say after that. We traded with Elsie for some supplies and left with the promise of a later reunion while dancing around the fact of whether there would be one.

Again we faced the weapon encrusted door. Zunk pronounced it safe and Tom gently pushed it open. Zunk’s judgement was less than accurate, sadly, and a host of spears appeared literally out of nowhere and caught all of us, Zunk taking the worst of it. I hope he did not think it his fault, I am sure he did the best he could, but it was an ill turn. Inside were a couple of acolytes and a metal man with a wand sticking out his chest, holding a wicked looking halberd.

The acolyte stings me with a couple of thrown daggers. Zunk toof great offense at this and slid in slicing the acolyte from guggle to zatch. I am fond of the little person for sure, but he is always quite messy. This time he was not so lucky and was doused with blood and gore.

Clever Hans, quickly noticing the similarities between metal man and the snakes earlier, fires an arc of lightening that sets the thing twitching and sparking, till it recovers and cast some kind of spell around it, likely for protection. The other acolyte attacks Zunk and trips in the process on an overreaching swing. I brain him hard, and hear Tom mutter; yeah fuck that guy. We are dazzled for a moment as metal man casts another spell and a spray of color issues forth; we see spots but suffer no other damage. The acolyte tries to rise and Tom sets him down for good. “Shoulda stayed on the ground, I tried to warn you” was all he said.

Zunk and I flank metal man, and I am desperate to take his attention away from my little friend, and a give it a mighty blow (maybe I am not so bad at this?). Hans has shifted to his force spell, but it’s not nearly as effective. The metal man is still attacking Zunk, probably because I am flailing uselessly. They are getting carved badly by the halberd and eventually go down as Tom is now in range. “Thomas”, I yell, “could you please fuck that guy?”. And he does, and I bring Zunk back around. It seems the wrong time to suggest a little thief should stay away from a guy with a big pole arm.

There is a statue of some sort of big brute in an alcove of the room, but little else. Other than being terribly ugly, it is distinguished by two features; glowing red gems for eyes, and a hole in the forehead that seems to be missing a horn. We recall the journal from other room with the chest and sure enough Zunk finds a hidden door in the alcove. Before we go, I lift Zunk up and ask him if he could pry out the gems; they seem wrong somehow. There is a change in the air as he does so, a feeling of evil that we can now detect only because of it’s absence. Well, at least we did some good here!

The door leads to downward stairs ending in a large natural cavern with several tunnels. We head down the southern one get surprised by dero. Hans and Zunk both get nicked by arrows that draw a little blood, but more importantly make them sluggish. I rush one of them to protect my friends and at least occupy its attention while we trade blows. Hans comes to the rescue with lightening, while Tom destroys the dero that had gotten behind Zunk, who swings one more time at the one engaged with me and wisely retreats. So proud of my little friend! The dero strikes me hard and it really hurts and it makes me so mad I swing at its head hard as I can and lay it out.

Before I can feel too bad about killing the dero, I look around and see what looks like a dead gnome in a cage (Holy cow, I hope it is not a friend of Hans) and another small person on a spit over a fire. They were eating people!

Aside from the big fire and the cage, there didn’t seem to be much else, until Zunk wanders into a side tunnel where two more dero are hiding. Sigh. Zunk takes a chain around the neck but gets out before he’s choked to death. There’s also some mushroom thing that sends out a bunch of spores. I heal Zunk from death’s door and they hit their foe, while lightening from Hans flickers over the dero. We might have gotten out of it until one of the dero conjured up a darkness that even our light could not pierce. Zunk is in big trouble-the last thing I saw was the two dero closing on him and I stumble to where I think he is, as Hans shouts to retreat. It’s not the worst idea, but I can’t leave Zunk. But I also don’t know where he is and the only thing I can do is send out an aura of healing. I run from the dark and the bloodthirsty dero. Hans is giving Zunk a dose of healing potion, but he still looks very bad between the effects of the spores and the choking cord of the dero.
